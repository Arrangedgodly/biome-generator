## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The specimen side is unmistakably authored for this product — a pixelated 512² map under the system's only shadow, a Surveyor's Probe reporting field-pixel coordinates and the classifier's live answer, staged Elevation → Moisture → Biomes reveal, and an accent (#20658c) borrowed from the map's own ocean. The instrument side (sliders, seed, preset chips, Generate) is closer to category-generic generator chrome, but deliberately quiet so the map stays the only voice — itself a product-specific choice. Specific where it counts, generic by intent where it doesn't.

**Deterministic scan**: 0 findings on `index.html` and `dist/index.html` (exit 0) — but in DEGRADED regex mode (htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils absent from disk), so custom properties, selector matching, and computed contrast were not evaluated: an undercount, not a clean bill. Static coverage is also thin by architecture: the markup entry is a 41-line skeleton; the control-panel DOM is runtime-built, so the CLI scan sees almost nothing of the real UI.

**Visual overlays**: Not available this run. Both subagents were refused browser access ("Browser is not available in subagent"), and the parent's page-evaluate surface is read-only (mutation rejected: "Possible side-effect in debug-evaluate"). No script injection, no in-page overlay, no console capture — fallback signal: HTTP probe (200, expected skeleton) plus the parent's fresh rendered verification from the polish pass an hour earlier (same working tree, zero edits since), which corroborates composition at 1080/800/375px, hairline contrast, seed hint, and share flash.

## Overall Impression

The craft floor is genuinely high — the error states and visual restraint would pass review on most product teams. But the critique surfaced a fault line between what the design PROMISES and what it DELIVERS: the moisture-slider-re-names-the-pixel coupling (product principle #1) is unreachable, the preset chips and the share URL tell different stories about which preset is active, and the map's meaning (12 unlabeled biomes) is locked behind luck-of-the-hover. The single biggest opportunity: make the probe and the map legible without discovery — that's where this goes from beautiful tool to self-explaining one.

## What's Working

1. **The Surveyor's Probe** (`src/ui/canvas-probe.ts`): DOM-only crosshair that never touches canvas pixels, field-resolution-aware coordinates, keyboard probing with name-first screen-reader sentences, pointer moves kept silent (no live-region chatter). The most product-specific interaction in the codebase.
2. **System discipline** (`src/style.css`): exactly one shadow, cut-out inputs that read as holes in the panel, min-height caption lines that never shift layout, 150ms chrome transitions. Few projects hold one rule this hard.
3. **Error-state craft** (`src/ui/control-panel.ts` `showError`): disabling the panel hands focus to Try again instead of stranding keyboard users on `<body>`; plus the share triad (clipboard → execCommand → visible fallback) with aria-live "Link copied" flash.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The advertised probe/moisture coupling is unreachable.** DESIGN.md promises "moving the moisture slider re-names the pixel under the crosshair — that is the point," but `report()` only fires on pointermove/focus/keydown. Moving the slider requires leaving the canvas — and `pointerleave`/`blur` clear the readout and hide the crosshair first. The readout is a snapshot, never refreshed on bias changes; product principle #1 silently doesn't exist. **Fix**: remember last fx/fy in the probe, subscribe to controller `state` events, re-`report()` when moisture changes while a reading is showing. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`.
2. **[P1] Two sources of truth for the active preset contradict each other.** `syncPressedPreset` re-derives pressed chips from 2-decimal slider positions; `app.ts` tracks a sticky `activePresetId` cleared only by override loss. Highlands + moisture nudge → chip visually unpresses but the shared URL still carries `preset=highlands` with terrain overrides; Highlands + elevation drag-and-return → chip re-presses but the URL is custom. Visible state and shared state disagree in both directions — corrosive for a tool whose core promise is "the link reproduces what you see." **Fix**: drive the pressed chip from the same sticky-id + `hasOverrides` signal (single source) exposed through the app ports. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`.
3. **[P1] The map's meaning is locked behind discovery.** 12 biome colors with no legend anywhere, slider labels with no axis explanation, an empty probe readout with no hint it exists, no pointer to the README/evidence. Stated success criterion #1 is understanding in 30 seconds; currently understanding depends on hovering the right pixel by luck. **Fix**: collapsible 12-swatch legend built from `BIOMES` (data exists), a quiet placeholder in the reserved readout line ("Hover the map to survey a pixel — arrow keys work too"), one quiet link to the repo. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable document`.
4. **[P2] Export PNG mid-reveal captures a half-blended frame.** `onExport` calls `toBlob` on the live canvas without settling the animation — during a crossfade the canvas holds a blend of two stages, so exporting at second 1.5 gets an artifact PNG named like a final one. **Fix**: skip/settle the animation before `toBlob`, or export the composed biomes surface. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`.
5. **[P2] Animation economy punishes exploration.** Every generate-class arrival — including every preset click and a same-state Generate — replays the full 2.8s reveal; the only filled control on the page usually delivers a byte-identical map plus a rerun, and preset A/B comparison (the core tuning loop) is taxed ~2.8s per click unless the user has learned Skip. **Fix**: animate only seed-changing generates; land preset switches instantly like elevation commits; optionally badge Generate when state is unchanged. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable animate`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user)**: Clicks Generate once, gets the same map, concludes the button is decorative — the real "new world" affordance is a 2.5rem icon button (🎲) with only an aria-label. Comparing presets costs 2.8s each or learning Skip. No keyboard accelerators at all (no R to roll, no 1/2/3 for presets).

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: The probe is genuinely good (focus announces a name-first center reading; arrows step; polite announcer). But the map's content is structurally invisible: `#map-canvas`'s aria-label is static and there is no generated-world summary — a non-visual user can interrogate one pixel at a time but never learn "this map is mostly ocean with a large western landmass." Four polite live regions (`#stage-label`, `#probe-announce`, `#seed-error`, `#share-status`) can fire during one reveal.

**Riley (stress tester)**: Hand-edits `#v=1&seed=abc&el=0.9` → `parseHashBody` resets the WHOLE state to defaults (el lost silently). A stationary hover readout never updates when the moisture slider moves (issue 1). Probe coordinates silently re-grid 512↔256 during elevation drags. Exporting mid-reveal yields a blended-frame PNG (issue 4). Refresh mid-animation replays cleanly; rapid preset spam is epoch-guarded and safe.

## Minor Observations

- Seed error renders "between 0 and 999999999" — unformatted; "999,999,999" scans faster.
- Preset pressed state is a 1px border-color change only — weak visible differentiation; consider a subtle inset or check treatment.
- The dice emoji is the only polychrome element in otherwise two-tone chrome; sanctioned by DESIGN.md but renders differently per platform.
- `#error-region` is appended last in the panel — potentially under the fold on short viewports at the moment it matters most.
- No busy indicator in the beat between Generate/dice and the first stage frame (canvas sits blank under its shadow).
- The stage label lingers at "Stage: Biomes" forever after completion; a settled state ("Map ready · seed 128") would reinforce the determinism promise.
- Nothing visible signals that clicking the canvas skips the reveal (no title/cue).
- `#map-canvas` has no `touch-action` policy, so touch-drag probing on mobile may fight page scroll.
- Slider readout spans are visual-only; `aria-describedby` would tighten the AT pairing.

## Questions to Consider

- If a recruiter spends 30 seconds and never hovers the map, the product's best moment never happened — what would a one-time, reduced-motion-safe self-demonstration of the probe (a brief crosshair sweep after the first reveal) cost the quiet?
- Determinism makes "Generate" the least generative control on the page — should the primary filled action actually be the dice/new-seed, with an honest "Replay reveal" secondary?
- The chrome borrows its accent from the map's ocean, yet the accent stays fixed while every seed re-mixes the palette — is fixed-ocean the right determinism story, or should the lamp re-derive from the current map's dominant biome?
