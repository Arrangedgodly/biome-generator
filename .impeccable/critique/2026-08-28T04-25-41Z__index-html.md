## Design Specificity Verdict

Accent IS the map's ocean hex; Loud Specimen / Single Shadow / Cut-Out rules are about the specimen; staged reveal with input-cancel + click-skip is genuinely this product's own. ## What's Working

1. Staged reveal engineered as first-class interaction (announce-once, sub-100ms skip, input-cancel, settled-before-done, reduced-motion bypass that still announces).
